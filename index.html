HOW TO PLAY

MOVE — arrows / WASD or drag the left pad.
JUMP — Z / K / SPACE. Hold for height, double-jump in air.
DASH — C / L / SHIFT. Air-dash once per jump. Dodges through attacks.
SLASH — X / J. The saber reflects some enemy bolts!
BOLT — hold to charge, release to fire.
◆ Scrap drops from foes. Spend it at beacons (green pads) on HP / EN / lives.
